About Me

Curious Mind. Builder Mindset.

I am Mayur Singh, a first-year B.Tech student specializing in Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning at SRMCEM, Lucknow. I am deeply focused on building a strong and structured foundation in computer science, with particular emphasis on programming, data structures, and problem-solving.
